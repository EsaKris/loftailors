Size guide

Find your size.

Joggers are sized by your waist. Measure yourself, find your range in the chart, then pick that size in the order section.

How to measure

  1. Waist. Wrap a soft tape around your natural waist, just above the belly button. Keep it snug, not tight.
  2. Hips. Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  3. Thigh. Measure around the fullest part of one thigh, close to the groin.
  4. Length (inseam). Measure from the crotch down the inside of the leg to where you want the joggers to end.

No soft tape? Measure a pair of trousers that fit you well, laid flat. Double the waist width to get the waist measurement.
